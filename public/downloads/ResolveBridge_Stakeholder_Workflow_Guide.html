3 End-to-End Operational Workflows

Step-by-step lifecycle execution illustrating how data and capital flow seamlessly between participants.

Workflow A: Personal & SME Credit Marketplace
Borrower ↔ Lender Direct
1
Instant Onboarding & Digital KYC
The borrower registers via mobile or web, providing national identity credentials (Ghana Card / BVN). OCR validation and biometric checks establish instant identity verification.
API: POST /api/v1/Auth/register & /api/v1/Users/kyc
2
Dynamic Credit Health Scoring & Priority Matching
The system computes the user's Health Index score based on verified cash flows and historical repayments, querying participating lenders for tailored product offerings.
Algorithm: Dynamic Weighted Health Score Engine
3
Multi-Lender Comparison & Offer Acceptance
The borrower reviews competitive interest rates, tenures, and monthly repayment schedules, selecting the best loan offer with transparent fee disclosures.
Module: /portal/apply-loan & /portal/marketplace
4
Automated Disbursal & Direct Debit Mandate
Upon administrative sign-off, capital is disbursed directly into the borrower's Mobile Money wallet or bank account. A recurring billing schedule is bound to the payment node.
Integration: Paystack Transfers & MTN MoMo API
Workflow B: Vehicle Financing & Dealer Escrow Ecosystem
Dealer ↔ Buyer ↔ Bank ↔ Insurer
1
Dealer Inventory Upload via Tokenized Portal
Certified auto dealers list verified vehicles using secure upload tokens, inputting VIN, valuation appraisals, inspection certificates, and pricing parameters.
Portal: /dealer-upload/[token] & /admin/vehicles
2
Customer Application & Collateral Packaging
Prospective buyers select their vehicle, input down-payment percentages, and generate a bundled auto-finance request.
Route: /portal/apply-vehicle/[id]
3
Dual-Approval: Asset Loan + Comprehensive Insurance
The partner bank approves financing against the vehicle collateral while an insurance partner simultaneously binds a comprehensive motor policy.
Module: /admin/bank-integration & /admin/insurance-integration
4
Escrow Payout to Dealer & Cryptographic QR Handover
Disbursement is held in escrow until delivery sign-off, after which funds are released to the dealer and a verified QR Cover Badge is generated for the vehicle owner.
Verification: /verify/policy & AES-256 Stamp
Workflow C: Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Point-of-Sale
Merchant ↔ Customer ↔ ResolveBridge
1
Point-of-Sale BNPL Selection
Customer scans the merchant's QR code or chooses ResolveBridge at web checkout. Instant credit line eligibility is calculated in real time.
Module: /portal/apply-bnpl
2
Split Payment Schedule Execution
Customer pays 25% down-payment via Mobile Money. Remaining installments are locked into a 3 to 12-month zero-hassle repayment plan.
Schedule: Automated MoMo Sweep Engine
3
Guaranteed Merchant T+1 Settlement
The merchant receives 100% payout upfront (minus platform discount fee), eliminating all default risks for the seller.
Console: /admin/merchant-console
