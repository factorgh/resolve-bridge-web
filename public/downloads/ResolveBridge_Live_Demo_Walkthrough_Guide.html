Audience: Retail Consumers, SME Entrepreneurs, Commercial Borrowers

1 The Public Discovery & Search Engine
Route: /
👁️ What to Show
  • Show the multi-vertical search tabs: Loans, Insurance, BNPL, Vehicles.
  • Highlight tier-1 Ghanaian bank badges: GCB, Fidelity, Stanbic, CalBank, Absa.
  • Demonstrate the real-time interest calculator slider on the homepage.
🗣️ What to Say (Pitch Script)

"In emerging markets, finding a competitive loan or insurance policy means visiting multiple bank branches and waiting weeks. ResolveBridge turns that into a unified digital marketplace where top-tier institutions compete for the borrower in real time."

✨ Key Wow Factor: Borrowers see transparent, pre-vetted rates across 6+ commercial banks in one central dashboard.
2 Digital Onboarding & Single-Window KYC
Route: /get-started
👁️ What to Show
  • Click through the 4-step onboarding flow: Financial Goal ➔ Employment/Income ➔ National ID (Ghana Card / BVN) ➔ Target Amount.
  • Show how the account is instantly created with zero physical paperwork.
🗣️ What to Say (Pitch Script)

"With a single 60-second digital KYC verification, the borrower builds a portable financial passport that unlocks loans, credit lines, and insurance across all our institutional partners."

3 Credit Health Index & Command Center
Route: /portal
👁️ What to Show
  • The Credit Health Index Gauge (0–100%) with customized diagnostic coaching.
  • Explain how positive repayment behavior dynamically improves the user's score.
  • Click into /portal/features/priority-loan-matching.
🗣️ What to Say (Pitch Script)

"Rather than a punitive black-box credit score, borrowers receive actionable coaching through our Health Index, showing them exactly how to qualify for lower interest rates and higher credit limits."

4 Instant Loan Application & MoMo Disbursal
Route: /portal/apply-loan
👁️ What to Show
  • Enter Loan Amount (e.g. GH₵ 20,000) and Duration (12 months).
  • Compare live bank rates: Stanbic VAF 18.5%, CalBank 20.0%, GCB 21.5%, Fidelity 22.0%.
  • Select preferred bank offer ➔ Choose Mobile Money / Bank payout ➔ Submit.
🗣️ What to Say (Pitch Script)

"The application is routed directly into the chosen bank's underwriting vault. Once approved, the funds hit the borrower's MTN MoMo wallet with automated monthly debit sweeps."
